Surface container behind panels, rows and modals. Resting elevation lifts to `--sh-md` when `interactive`; `glass` turns on the blurred translucent chrome used by nav bars and modals.

```jsx
<Card>面板內容</Card>
<Card inset padding={10}>子面板</Card>
<Card interactive>可點擊列</Card>
<Card glass>毛玻璃浮層</Card>
```

Props: `inset` (surface-2), `glass`, `interactive`, `padding`.
